Wholesale Ordering FAQs

Quick answers to our most common buyer and distributor questions.

Standard production time is 5 business days for 1-color and 2-color screen-printed non-woven bags after your virtual artwork proof has been officially approved. Full-color "Color Evolution" digital transfers take 10 business days. Rush 3-day production is available on select stock colors.
Our standard MOQ is 100 bags for grocery totes and 50 bags for heavy canvas totes or insulated cooler bags. Bags are shipped in master case packs of 50 to 100 bags to prevent transit damage.
