Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

At A Reusable Bag (areusablebag.com), we respect the privacy of our business clients, supermarket partners, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines the information we collect and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you request a quote, order free physical sample kits, or communicate with our sales team, we collect business contact details such as company name, recipient name, business email address, corporate phone number, commercial shipping address, and proprietary artwork files.

2. How We Use Information

We use this data exclusively to generate accurate wholesale price estimates, verify business eligibility for free sample kits, produce virtual artwork proofs, manufacture custom goods, and coordinate freight deliveries. We never sell, rent, or monetize your contact or business data.

3. Artwork & Trademark Protection

Any logos, vector files, and trademarks submitted to areusablebag.com remain the exclusive intellectual property of the submitting client or brand. We never reproduce your trademarked graphics for third parties without prior written consent.

4. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact us at privacy@areusablebag.com or call (800) 555-BAGS.
