Technical Preparation

Artwork & File Specifications

To guarantee your bags emerge crisp and legible from our press lines, please adhere to our prepress standards when submitting art files.

1. Vector Formats (Preferred)

Vector art allows infinite scaling without pixelation. We accept .AI (Adobe Illustrator), .EPS, .PDF, and .SVG files.

2. Convert All Fonts to Outlines / Curves

In Adobe Illustrator, select all text and use Type > Create Outlines (Ctrl+Shift+O). This eliminates font substitution errors when our prepress department opens your artwork.

3. High-Resolution Raster Artwork

If providing raster images (such as for Color Evolution CMYK transfers or photos), files must be minimum 300 DPI at 100% final imprint size (.TIFF or transparent .PNG).

4. Line Weight & Negative Space

Due to the textured dimpled surface of non-woven polypropylene, positive lines should be at least 1.0 pt thickness and reverse (negative) lines at least 1.5 pt to prevent ink fill-in.

5. PMS Spot Color Matching

Specify Pantone Solid Coated (PMS C) numbers for exact screen-print ink formulation. Note: Printing light-colored inks on dark-colored bags may have subtle tonal variations due to fabric absorption.
