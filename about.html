Material Science

Why Non-Woven Polypropylene (#5)?

Non-woven polypropylene (NWPP) is produced by spinning polypropylene polymer resin into fine continuous filaments and thermally bonding them together with patterned rollers. Unlike woven fabrics, it requires no weaving or spinning mills, dramatically reducing energy and water footprint.

Classified under Resin Identification Code #5 (PP), our bags can be completely re-melted and extruded into shipping pallets, automotive components, and recycling containers at the end of their lifecycle.

  • ✓ BPA-Free & Food Contact Safe: Safe for direct contact with fruits, vegetables, and bread.
  • ✓ Water & Mildew Resistant: Doesn't rot or absorb grocery spills like unlaminated paper.
  • ✓ Washable & Wipe-Clean: Can be sanitized with disinfectant wipes or cold hand-wash.
