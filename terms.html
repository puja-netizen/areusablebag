Wholesale Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: 2026

1. Wholesale Orders & Case Pack Increments

Products are packaged in master case cartons (typically 100 bags per box for non-woven grocery totes, 50 bags for canvas and coolers). All orders must meet minimum order quantity (MOQ) thresholds and conform to whole case pack quantities.

2. Virtual Proof Approvals & Lead Times

Production lead times (standard 5 business days for screen print, 10 business days for Color Evolution) commence on the next business day following written customer approval of the digital art proof and credit authorization.

3. Overruns / Underruns Policy

In accordance with standard commercial bag manufacturing practices, orders are subject to an overrun or underrun tolerance not to exceed +/- 5%. Clients are invoiced strictly for the exact quantity produced and shipped.

4. Custom Imprinted Goods & Returns

Custom imprinted merchandise cannot be returned or cancelled once production has started, except in instances of demonstrable factory defect or manufacturing discrepancy from the approved digital proof. Defect notifications must be submitted in writing within 10 business days of delivery.
