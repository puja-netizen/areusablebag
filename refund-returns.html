Refund and Returns Policy

Effective Date: 2026 | For wholesale and bulk custom orders only.

1. Custom Imprinted Merchandise — No Returns

All custom imprinted reusable bags, totes, insulated coolers, and wine carriers manufactured by A Reusable Bag to a client's approved artwork proof are non-returnable and non-cancellable once production has commenced. By submitting a signed approval of the digital art proof, the client acknowledges the order has entered production.

This policy applies to all screen-printed, Color Evolution CMYK digital transfer, and sublimated PET orders regardless of quantity, size, or number of imprint colors.

2. Manufacturing Defect Claims

A Reusable Bag will replace or credit orders containing verifiable factory defects, including:

  • Imprint misalignment exceeding 0.25" from the approved proof position
  • Missing or incomplete ink coverage (not attributable to fabric texture variation)
  • Handle seam failures on delivery (not damage from misuse or overloading)
  • Incorrect bag color delivered versus the approved color specification
  • Quantity shortfalls beyond the standard +/- 5% overrun/underrun tolerance

Defect claims must be submitted in writing via email to claims@areusablebag.com within 10 business days of confirmed delivery. Claims submitted after this window may not be accepted.

3. Defect Claim Process

  1. Email claims@areusablebag.com with your order number, company name, and a description of the defect.
  2. Attach clear photographs showing the defect on a minimum of 5 representative samples from the shipment.
  3. Our quality assurance team will respond within 2 business days to acknowledge receipt.
  4. If the claim is approved, we will arrange a replacement production run or issue a credit note at our discretion. Rush replacement production (5-business-day) is offered at no additional charge for verified defect claims.
  5. We may request return of a sample defect unit via prepaid label for root-cause analysis. We do not require return of the full defective shipment.

4. Blank (Undecorated) Stock Orders

Undecorated blank bag orders may be returned within 30 days of delivery, provided bags are in original, unopened case-pack condition and the buyer contacts us in advance for a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number. Return freight is at the buyer's expense. Restocking fees of 15% apply.

5. Order Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled at no charge before an artwork proof has been generated. Once the artwork proof has been sent, a cancellation fee of $75 per color (screen setup) applies. Once the digital proof has been approved in writing and production has commenced, no cancellation is possible.
